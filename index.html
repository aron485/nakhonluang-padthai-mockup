Pad Thai Near You

The Pad Thai Colorado Springs Comes Back For

When you search for Pad Thai in Colorado Springs, you want noodles that taste like they were made by hand, not pulled from a warmer. At Nakhon Luang Thai Kitchen, every plate is stir-fried fresh in the back of our dining room at 805 Village Center Drive, just off the Rockrimmon area in northwest Colorado Springs.

Our kitchen is led by a chef from Laos, so the familiar favorites you love sit right alongside dishes you won't find anywhere else in town. Our Pad Thai is the perfect place to start: springy rice noodles, egg, crisp bean sprouts, green onion and a shower of crushed peanuts.

Dine in with us, swing by for pickup, or order online and have it ready when you are.

Good To Know

Pad Thai Questions, Answered

What protein can I get in my Pad Thai?
You can order it with your choice of protein: pork, tofu, mixed vegetables, chicken, beef, shrimp, salmon, combination seafood or duck.
Is your Pad Thai spicy?
It's mild as written, but every dish can be made to your desired spice level — just let us know when you order.
What's the difference between Pad Thai and Drunken Noodles?
Pad Thai is stir-fried rice noodles with egg and bean sprouts, topped with green onions and crushed peanuts — mild and nutty. Drunken Noodles are stir-fried flat rice noodles with onions, bell peppers, bamboo shoots and basil leaves in a spicy chili sauce, so they're bolder and hotter.
Is there a glass-noodle version of Pad Thai?
Yes — Pad Thai Woon Sen is made with vermicelli (glass noodles) with egg and bean sprouts, topped with green onions and crushed peanuts.
What's in the Signature Pad Thai?
Our Nakhon Luang Signature Stir-Fried Noodles (Pad Thai) are rice noodles stir-fried with egg, lemongrass, bean sprouts and grilled chicken, topped with ground peanuts and green onion.
Do you have a kids' Pad Thai?
We do — our Kid's Pad Thai comes with a juice box and one crab rangoon.
